I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Florida**, advised by **Prof. Mingyue Ji**. My research lies at the intersection of **information and coding theory**, **distributed computing**, and **large-scale computing systems**.

I develop coding-based and resource-aware methods for reliable and efficient distributed computation, with an emphasis on **straggler tolerance**, **heterogeneous resources**, **elastic worker availability**, and **communication-computation tradeoffs**. My work combines fundamental analysis with algorithm and system design, including experimental evaluation on AWS EC2.

Recently, I have also been exploring how these ideas can contribute to **communication-efficient edge intelligence** and **efficient inference for large AI models**, including transformer inference and KV-cache compression.

My expected Ph.D. completion is **May 2027**.
