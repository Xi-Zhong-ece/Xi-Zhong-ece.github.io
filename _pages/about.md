I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Florida**, advised by **Prof. Mingyue Ji**.

My research lies at the intersection of **information and coding theory, distributed computing, and large-scale computing systems**. I combine fundamental analysis with algorithm and system design to develop efficient, reliable, and resource-aware computing methods.

My current work focuses on **coded distributed computation**, including distributed aggregation, elastic computing, heterogeneous systems, and straggler tolerance. Recently, I have also been exploring how coding, compression, and communication-efficient design can improve **edge and large-model inference**, including transformer inference and KV-cache compression.

I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology and B.S. in Statistics from Guangxi Normal University.
