## Research Mentoring

**Samuel Lu**, High School Student Researcher, Rowland Hall, Salt Lake City (2024 – 2025)

Mentored an independent research project on straggler-tolerant coded elastic computing, covering Lagrange coding fundamentals, scheme design, and numerical evaluation. The work resulted in co-authorship on two IEEE ISIT 2025 papers:
*Uncoded Download in Lagrange-Coded Elastic Computing with Straggler Tolerance* and
*Dual-Lagrange Encoding for Storage and Download in Elastic Computing for Resilience*.
