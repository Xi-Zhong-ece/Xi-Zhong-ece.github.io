Professional Summary

My research lies at the intersection of information and coding theory, distributed computing, and large-scale computing systems. I combine fundamental analysis with algorithm and system design to develop efficient, reliable, and resource-aware computing methods, and evaluate them in practical computing environments. Expected Ph.D. completion: May 2027.

Experience

  • 2021 - present

    Gainesville, FL, USA

    Graduate Research Assistant
    University of Florida / University of Utah
    Research on coded distributed computing, elastic and heterogeneous cloud computing, and coded caching, advised by Prof. Mingyue Ji.
    • Distributed aggregation and function computation: developed coded methods for directly computing aggregate functions in distributed systems under stragglers, avoiding unnecessary recovery of individual intermediate results; established fundamental feasibility and recovery limits through necessary and sufficient conditions and explicit constructions.
    • Elastic and heterogeneous cloud computing: designed computation-assignment schemes for distributed systems with heterogeneous machine speeds, storage capacities, elasticity, and stragglers; established theoretical performance guarantees and implemented the schemes on AWS EC2, reducing completion time by up to 30% relative to prior coded assignments and by over 50% relative to uncoded baselines under stragglers.
    • Efficient content delivery and caching: developed coded caching constructions with substantially reduced subpacketization, addressing a key implementation-complexity bottleneck in coded content delivery.
  • 2024 - 2025
