Research Overview

My research develops coding-based and resource-aware methods for reliable, efficient distributed computation. I combine fundamental analysis — converse bounds, feasibility conditions, achievable constructions — with algorithm and system design, and evaluate the resulting schemes in practical computing environments.

Distributed Aggregation and Function Computation

Coded methods for directly computing aggregate functions in distributed systems under stragglers, avoiding the unnecessary recovery of individual intermediate results. This line of work establishes fundamental feasibility and recovery limits through necessary and sufficient conditions, together with explicit constructions that achieve them.

Elastic and Heterogeneous Cloud Computing

Computation-assignment schemes for distributed systems with heterogeneous machine speeds, storage capacities, elasticity, and stragglers. Beyond theoretical performance guarantees, these schemes were implemented on AWS EC2, reducing completion time by up to 30% relative to prior coded assignments and by over 50% relative to uncoded baselines under stragglers.

Efficient Content Delivery and Caching

Coded caching constructions with substantially reduced subpacketization, addressing a key implementation-complexity bottleneck that limits the practicality of coded content delivery.

Communication-Efficient Edge Intelligence

A current direction extends these ideas to modern AI inference systems — jointly accounting for download, computation, and upload latency when communication resources are shared, with interest in transformer inference at the edge and KV-cache compression.
