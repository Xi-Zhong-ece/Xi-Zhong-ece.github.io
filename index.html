Xi Zhong

Ph.D. Candidate, Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Florida

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering

University of Florida

Gainesville, FL, USA

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, advised by Prof. Mingyue Ji. I started my Ph.D. at the University of Utah in 2021 and moved with my advisor’s research group to the University of Florida in 2024. Before that, I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology (advised by Prof. Minquan Cheng) and my B.S. in Statistics from Guangxi Normal University. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in May 2027.

My research lies at the intersection of information and coding theory, distributed computing, and large-scale computing systems. I combine fundamental analysis with algorithm and system design to develop efficient, reliable, and resource-aware computing methods, and evaluate them in practical computing environments. Specific directions include:

  • Distributed aggregation and function computation. Coded methods for directly computing aggregate functions in distributed systems under stragglers, with fundamental feasibility and recovery limits established through necessary and sufficient conditions and explicit constructions.
  • Elastic and heterogeneous cloud computing. Computation-assignment schemes for systems with heterogeneous machine speeds, storage capacities, elasticity, and stragglers, with theoretical guarantees and implementations on AWS EC2 that reduce completion time by up to 30% relative to prior coded assignments and by over 50% relative to uncoded baselines.
  • Efficient content delivery and caching. Coded caching constructions with substantially reduced subpacketization, addressing a key implementation-complexity bottleneck in coded content delivery.

More recently, I have also been exploring how these ideas can contribute to communication-efficient edge intelligence and efficient inference for large AI models, including transformer inference and KV-cache compression.

I serve as a reviewer for IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, IEEE Transactions on Communications, EURASIP JWCN, and for ISIT, ICC, and ITW. You can find my publications on Google Scholar and my full CV here.
